Automatic device-aware assessment flow

After the outbound collector is connected, ConfigSentinel will first identify the actual vendor, model, operating system, release and enabled capabilities. It will then select only the reviewed read-only commands needed for that device's configuration, security, operations, topology and approved control requirements. Unsupported or inaccessible checks will remain UNKNOWN—not PASS.
